Safety Procedures
All workers & staff used safety 
helmets while working on site.
Material is lifted with the help of 
proper chain pulley blocks and 
proper chain thickness is also 
checked for lifting the weight.
For avoiding major miss-
happening piping is segmented 
in to different sections and each 
section is tested hydraulically 
separately.
Welding is done only after 
ensuring that no inflammable 
material is lying nearby and fire 
extinguishers are available.
During welding proper sunglass 
is used for the safety of eyes and 
gloves are used for hands.
