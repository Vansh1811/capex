# People page — atmospheric reference imagery

All frames in this directory are **third-party reference/atmosphere imagery**,
not Capex team photography and not portraits of any named Capex person. They
communicate the _idea_ of the people behind the work — planning, field
engineering, mechanical and electrical trades — and must never be captioned
or alt-labeled as Capex employees. The roster's hover plates and section
frames deliberately avoid portrait-style framing for exactly this reason.
Replace 1:1 by the same filename when approved Capex photography arrives.

## License notes

- All six frames are CC0 or Public domain — no attribution required. The page
  still renders an explicit "atmospheric reference imagery" caption on every
  frame; the hero carries a visible note distinguishing reference material
  from Capex documentation.
- Candidates considered but rejected during triage are kept (not committed)
  under `output/wm-candidates/` with `scripts/research_people_imagery.py` +
  `scripts/triage_people_imagery.py` documenting the process.

## Sizes (post-optimization)

Progressive JPEG, center-cropped to slot aspect, long edge 1200–1920 px,
q80–82. Re-crop and re-optimize after replacing any frame.
