## License notes

- **CC BY / CC BY-SA frames** require attribution if the site ships them
  long-term. They are placeholders until approved Capex photography lands
  (tracked as part of the Phase 7 media gate); if any remain at launch, add a
  footer credit line or swap them out. The page renders them with explicit
  "atmosphere, not Capex work" captions regardless.
- **CC0 frames** need no attribution.
